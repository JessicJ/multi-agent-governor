# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous
community. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and focus criticism on ideas and evidence;
- welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience;
- disclose conflicts of interest and uncertainty;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and evaluation integrity;
- accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and avoid harassment or retaliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberate deception,
and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject
contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban participants when necessary and proportionate.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's private
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form and prefix the report title with
`[Conduct]`. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. If the
private form is unavailable, contact a repository maintainer through their
GitHub profile without disclosing sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as practical, avoid
retaliation, and recuse themselves when they have a conflict of interest.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by common open source community
standards, including the Contributor Covenant, while using wording tailored to
this project's maintainer capacity and evaluation-integrity requirements.
